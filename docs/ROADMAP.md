# NOVA Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Product and engineering foundation
- Confirm MVP boundaries and deployment environment.
- Establish application structure, environment configuration, database migrations, and CI checks.
- Define threat model, privacy model, and role/permission matrix.

## Phase 1 — Social MVP
- Authentication and profile management.
- Feed, posts, comments, reactions, and follows.
- Privacy-aware feed and profile access.
- Reporting, blocking, admin moderation, and notifications.
- Arabic RTL and responsive layouts.

## Phase 2 — Communities and messaging
- Groups, pages, memberships, and events.
- Direct messaging and attachment handling.
- Notification preferences and moderation queues.

## Phase 3 — Video and creator experience
- Channels, video uploads, playback, and creator subscriptions.
- Storage lifecycle, upload validation, transcoding strategy, and abuse controls.

## Phase 4 — Marketplace and engagement
- Listings and seller profiles.
- Points, badges, and transparent reward rules.
- Auctions only after bidding, payment, dispute, and fraud requirements are approved.

## Phase 5 — Integrations and scale
- Optional AI features with explicit data-use controls.
- Performance, observability, accessibility, and security hardening.
- Load testing, backup/restore drills, and operational runbooks.

## Release gate

A phase is not complete until its acceptance criteria, automated tests, security review, and deployment checks pass. Features depending on external providers remain disabled until credentials, limits, costs, and terms are verified.
